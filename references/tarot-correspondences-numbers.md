# 十大奇谜物语

> I think the story of Ten is perhaps my favorite story in esotericism. I like the story of Twenty-Two, and the story of Seven, and the story of Sixteen. But it’s the story of Ten, or One to Ten, that I return to again and again.

> Where do we encounter ten? In our ten fingers, our ten toes, in the decimal system that grew from our ten digits. In the world of correspondences, we find ten at the heart of numerology, we find ten in the degrees of and number of days in each decan (a decan, remember, is one-third of each 30-degree zodiacal sign). Above all, we find ten in the Tree of Life. In its ten sephiroth we find the story of creation, endlessly repeating, growing from the seed of Kether to the fruit of Malkuth. But we’ll be talking much, much more about that in the next chapter

我认为数字十的奥义，或许是神秘学中我最喜欢的内容。

我也喜欢数字二十二、数字七、数字十六的寓意，但唯有数字十，也就是从一到十的序列内涵，让我反复回味、一再探究。

我们在哪里能见到十？

我们有十根手指、十根脚趾，十进制计数法也正源于人的十指。

在对应体系里，十是数字命理的核心；占星中，每个十度分区恰好对应十度、约十日时长（记住：一个十度分区是三十度星座的三分之一）。

最重要的是，十体现在生命之树中。

生命之树的十个源质，完整演绎了创世的法则，从本源冠位的种子，一路生长直至物质王国的果实，循环往复、生生不息。

关于这些内容，我们会在下一章展开更详尽的探讨。
